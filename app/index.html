About DesertCrop Navigator

👩‍🌾 Who it is for: farmers in Jodhpur, Barmer and Sikar districts of Rajasthan.

🌱 What it does: you answer 4 short questions about your field: place, usual crops, water and soil. The app then shows the best crops for next season and compares three crop plans: what you grow now, a plan that saves water, and a plan that builds soil.

🤝 How it helps: you see which crops suit your field this year and why, the usual yield in your district, and what to check with your KVK before changing crops. It works in Hindi and English, and keeps working without internet once it has opened.

🛰️ How it uses NASA data:

  • Rain, heat and soil moisture: NASA POWER, for your location (rounded to about 10 km) or a farmland point in your district. Checked each time you open the app with internet.
  • Field greenness: NASA's HLS satellite pictures (from Landsat and Sentinel-2) of farmland in your district, showing when fields are green or bare.
  • These feed the crop ratings: for example, in a dry year, crops that need little water rate higher.

Disclaimer

Ratings come from simple general farming rules, not local recommendations. They do not know your market, seed, pests or sowing dates. Check every plan with your Krishi Vigyan Kendra or agriculture university before acting.
